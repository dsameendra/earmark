Earmark 1.1.1

Fixes and polish 26 November 2026 Release page

  • The last lines of a transcript can now be scrolled into view. In the video layout they used to sit behind the player bar.
  • Speakers, Translate, Re-transcribe, Bookmarks, View options and the inspector cards open their panel right next to the button you used, inside the window. They no longer open somewhere else in the app.
  • Re-transcribe keeps its Start button in view while the options scroll.
  • Home now has the same margins as Queue, Meetings, Live and Library.
  • Buttons on the orange accent are readable in dark mode.
  • Links use Earmark's accent colour instead of system blue.
  • Speaker colours match between the inspector, the transcript and the Library.
  • Replacements has visible text fields and clear option checkboxes.
  • The Export sheet opens on its main action, not inside the preset field.
  • Smaller touches: the Dictation card explains how to set it up, colour swatches are easier to hit, Storage shows 0 KB instead of Zero KB, and the Settings window no longer shows a white strip at the top.

Existing installations can update through Settings, General, Check for Updates.

Earmark 1.1.0

Broader languages and translation Release page

Earmark 1.1.0 adds many more on-device speech and translation models, translates live captions on their own switch, and makes it easier to see what each model can do before you download it.

New speech models

  • Qwen3-ASR 0.6B and 1.7B for 30 languages, alongside the Sinhala specialist, which is unchanged.
  • Parakeet v3, Ultra and Redux for 25 European languages with word timing. Redux compiles on first use, which can take several minutes once.
  • Parakeet EOU, a small English streaming model that ends each utterance when you pause.
  • Nemotron 3.5 Streaming: native live captions in 28 languages.
  • Voxtral Mini Realtime: streaming captions and files in 13 languages (needs a larger Mac).
  • Cohere Transcribe: file transcription in 14 languages.
  • Canary 1B v2: file transcription in 25 European languages. Recognition only; it never translates.

New translation models

  • NLLB-200 now covers around 200 languages and scripts instead of six. It remains experimental and non-commercial.
  • MADLAD-400 now runs natively, for 27 languages including Sinhala.
  • OPUS direction packs for English with German, French and Spanish, in both directions. Pick a source and target, and see the combined size first.
  • Hy-MT2 (1.8B and 7B) and TranslateGemma (4B and 12B). Each offers only the directions that passed Earmark's quality checks: English with German, French and Spanish, and Japanese and Chinese into English. Hy-MT2 7B also does English to Japanese. TranslateGemma shows the Gemma terms and asks you to accept them before downloading.

Live translation and routing

  • Live has an independent Translate captions switch with source and target pickers and a readiness message, instead of depending on the caption appearance preset.
  • Your chosen translation engine is now honored everywhere, and translation lag is bounded: stale partial captions are dropped, finals are kept, and the original caption stays if translation fails.
  • Apple Speech readiness is checked for the language you actually selected, with Apple Translation packs shown separately from speech assets.

Models page

  • A compact page with recommendations and installed models, plus a searchable Add models browser grouped by family. Each entry shows its languages, streaming mode, size, memory needs and licence.
  • Add from Hugging Face accepts links to the exact reviewed checkpoints only, pinned to a fixed revision. Unknown repositories and branches are refused before anything downloads.
  • Custom licences such as Gemma and OpenMDW are named plainly and need your acknowledgement before a download starts.
  • Large models are loaded one at a time, so starting one releases idle ones.

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Existing installations can update through Settings, General, Check for Updates.

Earmark 1.0.0

First release 23 November 2026 Release page

Private, on-device transcription for Mac: files, live captions, dictation and meetings.

  • Transcribe audio and video in batches, choosing language, model and speakers before each run.
  • Live captions overlay, dictation into any app, and Zoom and Teams capture with an ask-first default.
  • Editor with a waveform scrubber, speaker and bookmark markers, original, translated and bilingual views, and find and replace.
  • Speaker identification you can re-run without losing edits, and re-transcribe in one or several languages.
  • Multilingual, including Sinhala (experimental), with optional voice processing for noisy recordings.
  • Export to TXT, Markdown, DOCX, PDF, JSON, CSV, SRT and VTT.
