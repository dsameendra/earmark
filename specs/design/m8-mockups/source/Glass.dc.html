Liquid Glass, tastefully
Glass for chrome. Paper for words.
Glass is reserved for things that float above content: sidebar, toolbars, player dock, overlays, HUDs. Transcript text always sits on a solid sheet. With Reduce Transparency every glass layer swaps to its solid twin.

Where glass goes
Glass budget: three layers per screen at most. The sidebar, one floating control group in the toolbar (never a full-width bar), and one transient floater: player dock, live overlay, HUD, prompt or popover.
Never on cards, inspectors, lists, settings, or on top of other glass. Solid surfaces come in two tiers: sheet and inset.
