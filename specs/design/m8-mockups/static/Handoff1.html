NameSpec in boardsSwiftUI mapping (verify at T31)

Solid twinopaque sheet, 1 px stronger lineselected when AccessibilityPolicy.usesSolidSurface
Budget: three glass layers per screen at most (sidebar; one floating toolbar group, never a full-width bar; one transient floater). Cards, inspectors, lists, settings and transcripts are solid in two tiers: Sheet (primary content, #FCFAF6 / #1B1A17) and Inset (secondary, #F7F3EB / #22211D). No glass on glass. Wrap adjacent glass in one GlassEffectContainer. Migration: existing GlassCard and GlassPanel in Components.swift put glass on content; replace their use on content with solid SheetCard/InsetCard and keep glass only for the three allowed layers. Reduce Transparency / Increase Contrast route through the existing AccessibilityPolicy.usesSolidSurface.
