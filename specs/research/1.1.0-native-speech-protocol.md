# Native speech overlap probe protocol

The first isolated-worker overlap probe uses two authored English sentences rendered by the locally installed macOS Daniel and Samantha voices at 160 words/minute. The second voice starts 3 seconds after the first; both are mixed at gain 0.6 into 16 kHz mono PCM. Preserve generation metadata, durations, hashes and exact text in the external fixture manifest. This synthetic fixture tests plumbing and simultaneous-word recovery; it is not a natural-meeting accuracy benchmark or a voice redistribution grant. Do not bundle the audio in Earmark.

Before inspecting decoded output, the initial probe floor is: recover two distinct speaker identities, permutation-invariant speaker-attributed WER <=25%, preserve non-negative input-relative segment/word intervals, and at least 2 seconds of cross-speaker segment overlap. Execute offline and with 0.5-second PCM feeds, require final flushing and no truncation, then verify reset/restart and malformed/unsupported request rejection. Treat these as an initial structural/semantic probe, with natural licensed overlap, speaker stability and sustained real-time performance still required before catalog activation.

The follow-up diagnostic uses licensed natural voices from `google/fleurs`, English test parquet at `168de341b3db6859a9bac1c50a2ef5e3b47647e0` (CC-BY-4.0). Verify the 401,722,686-byte parquet SHA-256 `6428a4d04d3aac29e16b45e039bb1470a8bd7aa334cf92f7984c9c520d1f234d`. Before running the model, select the first male and first female utterance in parquet order lasting 6-15 seconds; these are IDs 1904 and 1950. Normalize each to RMS 0.08 with maximum per-source peak 0.45, start the second at 3 seconds, and retain a frozen mix/reference manifest. This guarantees headroom without choosing clips or mixing parameters from decoded output. Apply the same initial probe floor above. The constructed mixture is not a natural-meeting benchmark and cannot erase the failing authored fixture or establish live overlap support. `make-fleurs-overlap.py` requires research-only PyArrow 19.0.1; no dataset/library is bundled with Earmark.
